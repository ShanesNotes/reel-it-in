## Selected Stories

### Story 1

- Source: [Google I/O 2026 announcements](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/ai/google-io-2026-all-our-announcements/)
- Plain-English headline: Google wants AI to stop just answering and start doing things for you.
- What happened: Google announced Gemini 3.5 Flash and a broad set of agentic tools across Search, creation, shopping, development, and everyday help.
- Why it matters: This is the moment AI starts feeling less like a chatbot and more like a helper embedded in products people already use.
- Dad question: When you say it can "act," what is it actually allowed to do?
- Status: Selected

### Story 2

- Source: [Microsoft Security: Defense at AI speed](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2026/05/12/defense-at-ai-speed-microsofts-new-multi-model-agentic-security-system-tops-leading-industry-benchmark/)
- Plain-English headline: Microsoft says AI agents helped find serious Windows security holes.
- What happened: Microsoft described an agentic security system that helped researchers find 16 Windows networking and authentication vulnerabilities, including four critical remote-code-execution flaws.
- Why it matters: AI is not just writing code now; it is starting to inspect the digital plumbing we all depend on. That could help defenders, but it also raises the obvious question of what attackers can do with similar tools.
- Dad question: If AI can find those flaws, can criminals use it to find them too?
- Status: Selected

### Story 3

- Source: [European Commission AI transparency consultation](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-opens-consultation-draft-guidelines-ai-transparency-obligations)
- Plain-English headline: Europe is moving toward labels for AI conversations and AI-made media.
- What happened: The European Commission opened feedback on draft AI Act transparency guidelines. From August 2, 2026, people in the EU are supposed to be told when they are interacting with AI or seeing certain AI-generated or manipulated content.
- Why it matters: The internet is about to need ingredient labels. The hard part is deciding what must be labeled, who checks it, and whether normal people will trust the labels.
- Dad question: Would a label make you trust something more, or just make you wonder what else is unlabeled?
- Status: Selected

### Story 4

- Source: [Anthropic acquires Stainless](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-acquires-stainless)
- Plain-English headline: Anthropic bought a company that helps AI agents connect to other software.
- What happened: Anthropic acquired Stainless, which builds SDKs, CLIs, and MCP server tooling that let developers and agents use APIs.
- Why it matters: Agents are only useful if they can reach real systems. The fight is shifting from who has the smartest model to who controls the connectors, tools, and rails around it.
- Dad question: Is this like giving the AI a phone book and a set of keys?
- Status: Backup / deep conversation
