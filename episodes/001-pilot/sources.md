## Verification Notes

- Favor primary sources when possible.
- Record publication dates for news items.
- Flag anything based on rumor, leaks, anonymous sourcing, or fast-changing policy.
- Google I/O is broad; choose only one or two concrete examples during recording.
- Microsoft's post is a company security blog; treat benchmark claims as Microsoft-reported, not independently proven.
- EU transparency obligations are still in guideline/feedback phase, with dates and details subject to final implementation.
